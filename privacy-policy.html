Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

About the Application

TrendShorts Automation is a privately operated tool for creating, managing, and uploading videos to YouTube channels authorized by the operator.

Information Accessed

With the account owner's authorization, the application uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and access information necessary to complete the publishing process, such as channel identification.

Use of Information

Google account authorization is used solely to operate the application's YouTube publishing features. The application does not sell Google user data.

Storage and Security

OAuth authorization tokens may be stored locally on the operator's computer to maintain authorized access. These tokens should not be publicly shared.

Revoking Access

Users can revoke access through their Google Account connections.
