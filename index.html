Automated video creation and publishing tools.

About TrendShorts

TrendShorts Automation is a privately operated application designed to help its owner create, manage, and upload short-form videos to authorized YouTube channels.

The application uses the YouTube Data API through Google OAuth authorization. It accesses YouTube accounts only after the account owner grants permission.

How It Works

TrendShorts assists with video production, title and description preparation, and uploading videos to authorized channels. The owner controls which channels are connected and approves content before public publishing.
